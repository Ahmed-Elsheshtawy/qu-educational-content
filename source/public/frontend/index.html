About Masdar

Masdar is a student-created platform designed to help Qatar University students easily access and share educational resources such as past exams, lecture notes, and study materials.

Our Mission

We believe that education is enhanced when students collaborate and share knowledge. Masdar provides a centralized hub where students can contribute and access quality academic resources, making learning more efficient and collaborative.

What We Offer

  • Past exam papers and solutions
  • Lecture notes and study guides
  • Course assignments and projects
  • Easy-to-navigate course catalog
  • Community-driven content

Disclaimer: This is an independent project built by students for students, and is not officially affiliated with or endorsed by Qatar University.

Support the Project

Masdar is a free platform maintained by students. Running this service requires:

  • Hosting and server infrastructure
  • API usage costs for file storage and retrieval
  • Domain registration and SSL certificates
  • Ongoing maintenance and development

Your support helps keep Masdar free and accessible for all Qatar University students. Even a small donation helps cover these costs and ensures the platform stays online.

Submission Guidelines

  • Only submit educational materials relevant to Qatar University courses

  • Uploaded files are stored securely in the cloud
  • All submissions are reviewed before being published
