About Masdar

Masdar is a student-created platform designed to help Qatar University students easily access and share educational resources such as past exams, lecture notes, and study materials.

About the Developer (Me)

My name is Ahmed Elsheshtawy. I am a Qatar University Student, currently pursuing a Bachelor's degree in Electrical Engineering. Click here to learn more about me.

My Mission

I believe that education is enhanced when students collaborate and share knowledge. Masdar provides a centralized hub where students can contribute and access quality academic resources, making learning more efficient and collaborative.

What I Offer

  • Past exam papers and Quizzes
  • Lecture notes and Useful Explanations
  • Course assignments and projects
  • Easy-to-navigate course catalog
  • Community-driven content

Disclaimer: This is an independent project built by a student for students, and is not officially affiliated with or endorsed by Qatar University.

Support the Project

Masdar is a free platform maintained by a student. Running this service requires:

  • Hosting and server infrastructure
  • API usage costs for file storage and retrieval
  • Domain registration
  • Ongoing maintenance and development

Your support helps keep Masdar free and accessible for all Qatar University students. Even a small donation helps cover these costs and ensures the platform stays online.

Submission Guidelines

  • Only submit educational materials relevant to Qatar University courses
  • Ensure you have the right to share the file
  • Use clear, descriptive titles
  • Either upload a file or provide a public URL (not both)
  • Uploaded files are stored securely in the cloud
  • All submissions are reviewed before being published
